generated by `scripts/make_examples.py`. Citations give document, locator and printed page (with the PDF page when
they differ). "verified" means the deterministic checks passed: every quote is in its source and every number is in

The gate line is the fine-tuned cross-encoder's best score. Below the threshold the answer is flagged for review;
it does not change the answer. The threshold was chosen on synthetic queries and flags some correct answers to
real, longer questions (DESIGN.md section 6), so treat it as a review signal, not a verdict.
